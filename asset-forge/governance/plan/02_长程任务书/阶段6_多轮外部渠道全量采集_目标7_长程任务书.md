## 目标

建立可复用的外部信息收集流程，尽可能广泛地整理官方仓库、官方文档、论文、数据/标注工具、替代训练器和公开元数据渠道，并通过多轮有界采集生成可审计的知识与模板候选。

## 范围与约束

- In scope: channel catalog, public retrieval, revision pinning, source deduplication, short factual extraction, candidate Markdown, validator-tested templates, eval seeds, coverage and migration preview.
- Out of scope: private/login sources, unrestricted crawling, model/image downloads, GPU training, source-code edits, formal migration, and claims that external configs are product-compatible.
